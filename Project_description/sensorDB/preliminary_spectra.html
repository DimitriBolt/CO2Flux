Method, coverage and limits

A = √(a²+b²) in C(t)=c+a cos(2πft)+b sin(2πft): equal-weight floating-mean sinusoidal semi-amplitude, not normalized power. No interpolation, regularization, unit conversion, taper, trend removal or phase calculation. Each fit uses its own real timestamps after the existing technical exclusions; doubtful retained observations remain flagged.

Frequency spacing is 1/T (static) or 1/window_days (sliding). The display uses f < 6 cycles/day, half the 12/day slow modal air cadence; the exactly 6/day endpoint is excluded because nearly regular 2-hour segments cannot stably determine both sinusoidal coefficients; irregular observations have no single strict Nyquist frequency. A denser grid is not used. Dashed line: daily reference, not a preselected conclusion. Check the actual sampling window for possible aliases.

Sliding windows are half-open and recalculated from the embedded observations when selected or played; changing window/step rebuilds the entire date sequence and clears calculated spectra. A frequency needs ≥1 cycle within actual observed span, ≥4 distinct times and a nonsingular fit. Otherwise it is omitted with INSUFFICIENT DATA; these are numerical display rules, not new scientific admission criteria. Counts, occupied days / 2-hour bins, internal gaps and missing window edges are shown. Partial bins count as occupied; they are not simultaneous measurements. Large gaps alone have no newly invented exclusion threshold. Short windows cannot establish annual periodicity.

The full 521-day candidate calendar has 520 occupied four-channel days in two runs (29 Apr–11 Aug 2025 and 13 Aug 2025–1 Oct 2026). It is not a continuous validated series. All LI-COR observations have unresolved numeric QC; source time zone / clock alignment are unconfirmed. Air horizontal offset: 1 m. Air Excel Z=0.25 m and Oracle BOXZ=0.05 m disagree. Source units stay separate.
